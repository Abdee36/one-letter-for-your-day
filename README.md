One letter, A to Z, standing for the word that describes your day. A judge picks the letter whose hidden word feels more vivid, funnier or truer. Free to enter, $5 shared by the top letters. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "One Letter for Your Day" bounty.

## Entry

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network) writes exactly one letter to `/output/entry.txt`:

> E

E for **Exhausted** — the day that wrung everything out of me and left nothing in the tank. Vivid, honest, and a little funny in its bleakness: the kind of day you report with one tired letter.

Run locally:

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
